THE OPERATION

What Was Operation Dragoon?

Allied forces landing in Southern France

Operation Dragoon was an Allied military operation during the Second World War. It began on 15 August 1944 with a large-scale amphibious landing on the Mediterranean coast of Southern France.

The operation was designed to establish a strong Allied position in southern France, capture important ports and support the Allied advance across Europe.

Thousands of Allied soldiers, supported by naval forces and aircraft, took part in the operation. French forces also played an important role in the campaign.

The invasion was supported by extensive naval bombardment and Allied air operations. After securing the landing areas, Allied forces moved inland and captured important towns and communication routes.

The campaign helped create another major Allied route into France while German forces in the region were forced to retreat.

MISSION

Objectives of Operation Dragoon

01

Capture Ports

Secure important Mediterranean ports to support Allied logistics and supply operations.

02

Open a New Front

Establish another major Allied front in France and place additional pressure on German forces.

03

Advance North

Move north through France and connect with Allied forces advancing from other directions.

04

Support Liberation

Help liberate more French territory from German occupation.
